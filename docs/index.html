Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Staff Attendance & Payment ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit your personal information to any server. The app uses Google AdMob for advertisements, which may collect device identifiers for ad personalization.

1. Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Staff member names and details you enter
  • Attendance records
  • Payment and salary information
  • Profile photos (if added)
  • App settings and preferences

This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It remains entirely on your device.

Information Collected by Third Parties

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data

This data is collected by Google according to their privacy policy. For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Information

  • Local Data: Used solely to provide app functionality (tracking attendance, calculating salaries, etc.)
  • Advertising Data: Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements

3. Data Storage and Security

All your staff and attendance data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods. We do not have access to this data.

Important: If you uninstall the app or clear app data, all locally stored information will be permanently deleted.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Advertising: Google AdMob receives device identifiers to serve ads

5. Your Choices

  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization)
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
